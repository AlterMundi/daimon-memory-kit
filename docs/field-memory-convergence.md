## What changed

Portable memory must support returning to a context and recognizing how that
return affects earlier understanding, as well as preservation and useful recall.
Nexus supplies the encounter and navigable expression; HMK supplies continuity,
recall and learning through its existing APIs. The original event, a subsequent
revisit and each participant's interpretation remain related and distinct.

Our feedback-prevention rule needs this semantic precision: rereading an old
proposal is not another occurrence or independent corroboration of that proposal.
An actual subsequent conversation, changed interpretation or newly recognized
question may itself be meaningful new experience. Conversely, a revisit need not
transform anything: confirmation, uncertainty and an open question are legitimate
outcomes. Mechanical rephrasing alone does not establish a new experience.

An analogy or imagined possibility can matter without being established as a
fact. Preserve the attributed encounter and its meaning; distinguish support for
what participants said or experienced from support for the analogy's truth.
Technical reconciliation does not require agreement among perspectives. A source
correction may require reviewing dependent interpretations without silently
rewriting another participant's account.

## Exact continuation

The memory qualification below is complete within its recorded scope. The next
integration-specific continuation is to map actual `/merge` delivery/acceptance
to received context, selected uptake and changed understanding, using the owning
Telecodex implementation. Provide source-version references and navigable links;
delivery alone must not claim incorporation. Qualify one return through that real
flow rather than repeating this memory test. Production stays manual.

Preserved qualification contract under
[HMK #11](https://github.com/nicoechaniz/hermes-memory-kit/issues/11): inspect
the existing capture, revision, support and projection APIs against one selected
case from the actual context exchange. Keep source material private; publish only
selected general design and qualification findings.

Establish whether existing operations can preserve:

1. The earlier understanding and its original source/event identity.
2. The later received contribution or encounter, with actual attribution and
   the source versions available to it.
3. What changed, was confirmed or remained open, and why, without inventing
   agreement or treating one source as several independent confirmations.
4. A later useful return: recover the distinction and its reasons from a weak
   clue, with enough retained meaning if the original context is unavailable.

If the contract already expresses these distinctions, reuse it. Otherwise record
the demonstrated gap and change only its owning component. Do not infer a schema
change or launch a broad benchmark from the conceptual framing. Deliberate
retention can establish semantics before an automatic capture path is qualified.
The new evidence concerns attributed learning through revisiting and human
usefulness; existing preservation/receiving evidence remains reusable.
