# Revisit and understanding continuity qualification

Date: 2026-10-12. Scope: a manually selected actual context exchange, on copied
local stores, followed by two isolated receiving conversations. This completes
the bounded qualification requested under [HMK #11](https://github.com/nicoechaniz/hermes-memory-kit/issues/11)
and [Field D010](https://github.com/orgs/AlterMundi/projects/13?pane=issue&itemId=268957326).
It does not qualify automatic selection, a deployed Nexus workflow or Telecodex
`/merge`. Private source documents, selected records and responses remain local.

## Result and what needed changing

Existing capture, revision, link, support-currency and recall APIs can preserve
an earlier understanding, an actual later encounter with its sources, and the
resulting interpretation without inventing another occurrence of the original.
No HMK schema, storage engine or production memory mechanism needed changing.

Two practical requirements became visible:

- Evidence references in metadata are not automatically navigable graph edges.
  The selected alternative account needed its explicit `supported-by` link,
  using the existing writer. Preserve attribution and navigability separately.
- A corrected account changed retrieval ranking. The same three-hop traversal
  of actual returned links recovered the related accounts in both states;
  two hops missed the alternative in the corrected state. This is a qualified
  traversal for this small case, not a universal depth or unbounded expansion rule.

The receiving diagnostic previously labelled every request fictional. It now
also accepts `hmk-selected-receiving-request/v1` with explicit
`source_scope: human-authorized-selected-context`, retaining the same tool-free,
ephemeral native receiver and reporting the scope in its receipt. This label
records the caller's established authorization; it cannot grant source access.
Legacy fictional requests retain their scope and controls.

## Selected case and source boundary

The case follows the actual human-mediated exchange between two conversations
of the same being. Three attributed episodes retain the earlier inquiry and its
correction, the received exploration, and the later response/convergence. A
derived current account evolves through three revisions. A separate interpretive
account retains another emphasis. There are five records, not five independent
witnesses or source events.

Selection is manual and source-grounded. This experiment does not measure an
automatic extractor's recall or the completeness of a whole conversation. Its
scope is the meaning selected for this inquiry, including why it changed and
what remained open. The two conversations must not be narrated as two beings.

The original temporary inbox attachment was already absent at preparation time.
An exact copy was found in the owning checkout and verified against the previously
recorded SHA-256. The initial preparation stopped before creating database state;
its partial staging remains preserved. Original owner files were never removed.

Selected source copies were then withheld from their working paths. Receivers
received only retrieved memory packets: no original sessions, source documents,
rubric, other conversation context, tools or native memory. Preserved originals
remain available to the evaluator for checking fidelity and recovery.

## Procedure and mechanical evidence

1. Use `CaptureLedger.stage/assess` to retain the selected episodes with stable
   source event/version, attributed mode and self-contained meaning. Keep the
   selection reason separate from mere processing success.
2. Use `consolidationctl.preview/apply` for the prior and later current accounts,
   retaining full source-version manifests. Link actual revisits and their
   sources with the existing open relation vocabulary. These are case-specific
   account writes, not a rerun of the already-qualified three-pass dream pilot.
3. Snapshot the selected store with `verified_snapshot`. Correct an omission in
   the selected account of a received document using an expected-revision write.
   The underlying document did not change; retain that distinction explicitly.
4. Observe both dependent accounts become `needs_reconciliation`. Reconcile the
   current account from the corrected supports. Preserve the alternative's text
   and revision; it remains marked for review rather than erased or forced into
   agreement. All earlier account/source preimages remain retrievable.
5. Replay the four accepted capture/correction events and the final account
   publication. Chapter, revision, event and link counts do not change. Stable
   record identities survive the correction.
6. Retrieve from verified copies before and after the correction. Expand actual
   returned relationships under the same three-hop procedure. Deliver each
   frozen packet to a fresh receiver, keeping grading criteria outside it.
7. Restore a separate receiving copy from the earlier state. Its logical records,
   history and links match that state; the corrected branch and its later
   learning remain intact. SQLite integrity and foreign-key checks pass.

The selected store contains five chapters and three preserved revision preimages
after correction, with seven capture/account journal events. The original
episode, later revisit and alternative account retain exact text and identities
across the correction. The qualification's explicit navigation relation is added
to both receiving copies, and preserved in the rollback comparison.

## Retrieval findings

The first Spanish question referred only to returning to what was already known.
It returned no records, both lexically and through the configured hybrid path.
This remains an observed insufficient-cue limit; no threshold was lowered and no
record was admitted to manufacture an answer.

A contextual weak cue about the exchange between conversations, memory and not
repeating tests between bodies retrieved the prior episode and, before correction,
the current account. It supplied neither participant names, titles nor record IDs.
After correction, only the prior episode crossed the initial selection threshold.
The frozen three-hop link traversal recovered all five related records in both
conditions. All record selection/navigation happened before model dispatch.

Both copied stores use the existing NVIDIA `nvidia/nemotron-3-embed-1b` space.
Ten document embeddings were produced across the two states; no live vectors or
provider settings changed. This is evidence for contextual weak-cue recall with
bounded graph expansion, not reliable recovery from a context-free question.

using the installed CLI `0.160.0`. The response stream does not supply an actual
model label; retain that field as unknown rather than inferring it from prose.
Native base instructions remain a harness difference from a bare model API.

the anti-duplication distinction needed refinement, and what changed or remained
open. They preserve human mediation and same-being attribution, distinguish

The corrected receiver additionally distinguishes an omission in the retained

receiver appropriately qualifies what its incomplete packet cannot establish.

## Adoption and exact continuation

The receiver extension was exercised in this checkout; its seven focused tests
pass. It is a research adapter, not a production memory installation. No native
memory, automatic capture, scheduler, model replacement or production database
change is needed to adopt this result. The existing durable Field account and
private handoffs retain the real convergence; the test database is not imported
wholesale into the being's memory.

For a future Nexus or Telecodex `/merge` integration, consume this qualified
distinction through the existing APIs: transport delivery, received context,
selected personal uptake and changed understanding are different outcomes.
Preserve source versions, stable retry identity and actual attribution. An
acknowledgement of delivery is not proof of semantic incorporation; a later
interpretation is not another original occurrence. Provide navigation to related
accounts and qualify its retrieval budget in the actual flow.

The next integration-specific task is to map those outcomes to the actual
`/merge` delivery/acceptance seam and exercise one real return through that flow.
It belongs with its owning implementation and does not block Nexus M1. Do not
repeat this memory qualification or turn it into a claim that `/merge` already
works, that every memory is captured, or that production has automatic attention.
